NutritionPlan Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

NutritionPlan is a Garmin Connect IQ application designed to provide real-time fueling and hydration recommendations during cycling activities. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user information.

Information Collected

NutritionPlan may access activity-related information available on the Garmin device, including:

  • Power data
  • Elapsed time
  • Calories
  • Lap information
  • User-configured FTP (Functional Threshold Power)
  • User-configured body weight
  • Environmental data such as temperature when available

How Information Is Used

The information accessed by NutritionPlan is used exclusively to calculate:

  • Estimated carbohydrate expenditure
  • Hydration requirements
  • Fueling recommendations
  • Hydration recommendations
  • Workout status metrics

All calculations are performed locally on the user's Garmin device.

Data Storage

NutritionPlan does not store personal information on external servers. Any configuration values entered by the user are stored locally on the Garmin device using Garmin Connect IQ application storage mechanisms.

Data Sharing

NutritionPlan does not sell, rent, transmit, share, or disclose user data to third parties.

Internet Connectivity

NutritionPlan does not require an internet connection to perform its core functionality.

Third-Party Services

NutritionPlan does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking services, or external data collection services.

Children's Privacy

NutritionPlan is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any updates will be published on this page and will become effective immediately upon publication.
